# univalent-category

A **univalent category** $\mathcal C$ is a [category](./category.md) where the dependent
product

$$\mathrm{idtoiso}\colon \prod_{X\colon \mathcal C} \prod_{Y\colon \mathcal C} (X = Y) \to (X \simeq Y)$$

is an equivalence.[^1][^2]
