# opposite-category

The **opposite category** $ \mathcal C^\mathrm{op} $ of a [category](./category.md) $\mathcal C$
is a category where
$\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal C^\mathrm{op}}(X, Y) :\equiv \mathrm{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(Y, X)$ and the
[identities](./category.md) and [morphisms](./category.md) are inherited from $ \mathcal C $.[^1]

## intuition

$ \mathcal C^\mathrm{op} $ is the same as $\mathcal C$, just with the directions of its morphisms
reversed.[^2]
